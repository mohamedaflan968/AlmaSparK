FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

How long does a typical project take?

A simple landing page takes 3–5 days. Business websites take 1–3 weeks. Complex web applications can take 4–8 weeks. We always share a clear timeline before we begin.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on your goals and scope. Starter websites start from $149, and full custom builds are quoted after a free consultation. No hidden fees, ever.

Do you provide hosting and domain?

Yes. We offer secure hosting with free SSL, daily backups and 99.9% uptime. We also help you register and configure your domain name.

Will my website be mobile friendly?

Absolutely. Every project is designed mobile-first and tested across all devices and browsers to deliver a flawless experience everywhere.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — every plan includes post-launch support. Our maintenance plans cover updates, security, backups and 24/7 monitoring.

How do we get started?

Simply hit "Get Free Quote" or contact us via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free consultation, understand your goals and send you a proposal within 24 hours.
